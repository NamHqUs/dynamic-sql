# Sales report catalog

The catalog below describes a broad sales-company demo, from pipeline and lead generation through customer retention, fulfillment, and profitability. Each report should use clearly defined sales measures and offer relevant filters such as date range, owner, territory, product, channel, and customer segment.

| Category | Report | What it helps answer |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue & management | Sales scorecard | How do revenue and bookings compare with target? |
| Revenue & management | Revenue trend and variance | How are sales changing over time, and where are we ahead or behind plan? |
| Revenue & management | Quota attainment | Which teams and reps are on track to meet quota? |
| Revenue & management | Win/loss reasons | Why are deals won or lost, and which competitors or objections recur? |
| Pipeline & forecast | Pipeline by stage | What opportunities and value sit at each stage? |
| Pipeline & forecast | Pipeline coverage | Is there enough qualified pipeline to meet future targets? |
| Pipeline & forecast | Stage conversion | Where do opportunities drop out of the sales process? |
| Pipeline & forecast | Aging and stalled opportunities | Which deals have been inactive or in a stage too long? |
| Pipeline & forecast | Forecast vs. actual | How accurate are forecasts, and how does performance compare with them? |
| Lead & demand | Lead-source quality | Which sources produce qualified opportunities and won business? |
| Lead & demand | Lead-to-sale funnel | How many leads progress through qualification, opportunity, and closed-won? |
| Lead & demand | Speed-to-lead and follow-up SLA | Are new leads contacted promptly and followed up as expected? |
| Lead & demand | Campaign contribution and ROI | Which campaigns contribute pipeline and revenue relative to spend? |
| Team execution | Sales activity vs. outcomes | Which calls, meetings, and follow-ups correlate with progress and wins? |
| Team execution | Rep performance | How do reps compare on revenue, win rate, deal size, and attainment? |
| Team execution | Sales cycle and deal velocity | How long do deals take, and what factors speed up or slow down closing? |
| Team execution | Overdue tasks and follow-ups | Which next steps are overdue, and what needs attention? |
| Customers & accounts | Customer revenue contribution | Which customers account for the most sales? |
| Customers & accounts | New vs. repeat customers | How much business comes from new customers versus returning ones? |
| Customers & accounts | Account growth and cross-sell | Which accounts are growing, and where might there be additional product opportunities? |
| Customers & accounts | Customer concentration and inactivity | Is revenue dependent on a few accounts, and which valuable accounts have gone quiet? |
| Product & economics | Sales by product or SKU | Which products generate the most revenue and units sold? |
| Product & economics | Product mix and attach rate | Which products are commonly bought together, and what is the mix of sales? |
| Product & economics | Discount and price realization | How much discount is given, and how does it affect achieved selling price? |
| Product & economics | Gross margin by deal, product, or segment | Which sales are most profitable, not just largest by revenue? |
| Territory & channel | Territory or regional performance | Which locations or territories are ahead or behind plan? |
| Territory & channel | Channel and partner performance | Which direct, partner, reseller, or other channels deliver results? |
| Territory & channel | Segment and industry performance | Which customer segments or industries convert and spend best? |
| Order execution | Open-order backlog and aging | What orders remain open, and which are becoming overdue? |
| Order execution | Order fulfillment and cancellations | How quickly are orders fulfilled, and how often are they delayed or cancelled? |
| Customer lifecycle | Upcoming renewals and renewal pipeline | Which contracts are due for renewal, what value is at risk, and who owns the next step? |
| Customer lifecycle | Retention, churn, and win-back | Which customers are retained or lost, why do they churn, and which can be won back? |
| Customer lifecycle | Reorder cadence and next expected purchase | Which repeat-purchase customers are overdue based on their usual reorder interval? |
| Inventory & fulfillment | Stock availability, backorders, and potential lost sales | Where are stockouts or backorders putting expected sales at risk? |
| Finance & profitability | Accounts receivable aging by customer and sales owner | Which customer balances are overdue, and who should follow up? |
| Finance & profitability | Customer contribution and cost-to-serve | Which customers remain profitable after service, freight, commissions, and returns? |
| Post-sale quality | Returns, credits, and cancellation reasons | Which products, customers, channels, or sales practices are associated with returns and cancellations? |
| Fulfillment quality | On-time, in-full delivery | Where are delivery delays or incomplete orders affecting customer experience and repeat sales? |

## Coverage review

The first 30 reports cover the core CRM view: targets, pipeline, lead conversion, rep execution, customers, products, territories, channels, and order status. The final eight add customer lifecycle, inventory availability, collections, cost-to-serve, returns, and delivery quality. Those areas are especially valuable for recurring-revenue, wholesale, and physical-goods businesses, and depend on the corresponding contract, inventory, finance, and fulfillment data being available.

For reliable comparisons, define whether “sales” means booked, invoiced, shipped, or recognized revenue, and use that definition consistently across reports.
